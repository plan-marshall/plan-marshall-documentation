= Use Cases: Plans (UC-PLN)
:toc: left
:toclevels: 2
:nofooter:

link:../UseCases.adoc[Use Cases] » Plans

The operator works with running plans and epics: follows them down to the part that needs attention, decides what a plan asks, and shapes the process a plan runs. The operator does this in the web app or in the terminal client; each use case says what the operator achieves, and how each client shows it is its design (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/README.adoc[`pm-mcp-clients`, Design]).

[#UC-PLN-1]
== UC-PLN-1: Follow a Plan Down to Its Parts

Status:: `DRAFT`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: follow a plan from its phases down to the step it is in, see at every level what is done, what runs, and what is still planned, and understand where it is and how it got there.
Trigger:: the operator selects a plan in the overview of running work (<<UC-PLN-4>>), or names it.
Flow::
. *The plan.* The plan's phases, in the order the plan runs them, each with its state: _done_, _running_, _planned_, or _waiting for the operator_; the phases its composition left out (another lane, a step the project does not need) are listed apart as _not in this plan_, with the reason. A phase the plan ran more than once (a loop-back) says how often, and the run it is in.
. *A phase.* Selecting a phase shows its workflow: its states in the order of the workflow, each with its state, and the sub-workflows it calls marked as sub-workflows, each with its state as a whole. Where the workflow branches, the branch taken is shown, and a branch not taken as _not taken_.
. *A sub-workflow.* Selecting a sub-workflow shows its workflow the same way, and so on through every level of nesting, down to the leaves: the states that call nothing.
. *A leaf.* A leaf shows what runs or ran in it: its job or its tasks (role, worker, attempt, outcome), its times, and its outcome; a state waiting for the operator leads into <<UC-PLN-2>>.
. *Where it is.* At every level the path from the plan down to the level shown is visible, and the way back up; the level the plan is at is marked, and the operator can jump to it.
Variations:: the view follows the plan as it runs: a state that is entered becomes _running_, one that is left _done_, and a new version of the plan's composition (an outline, a replan) changes what is planned, which the view shows as changed. A plan whose process changed after a state ran shows that state as it was when it ran. This use case is about plans; an epic has a view of its own (<<UC-PLN-5>>).
Outcome:: the operator knows, at every level of the plan's process, what is done, what runs, and what is still to come, and has found the part that needs attention.
Changes::
* *Phases as sub-workflows* (<<change-phase-sub-workflows>>).
* *The plan's process as a structure* (<<change-process-resource>>).
* *The plan's history* (<<change-plan-history>>).
* *Events of the process* (<<change-process-events>>).
Requirements:: link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (the dependable view of every plan); the composed plan is specified in link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-instance[Plan Instance], its sub-workflows in link:../specification/workflow-dsl/06-reusable-sub-workflows.adoc[Reusable Sub-Workflows].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/plan-view.adoc[Plan View] (what both clients show); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/16-plan-view.adoc[Terminal Client, The Plan View].

[#UC-PLN-2]
== UC-PLN-2: Decide What a Plan Asks

Status:: `DRAFT`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client (link:05-starting-work.adoc#change-clients-only[All Interaction in the Operator's Clients]).
Goal:: learn at once that a plan or an epic waits for a decision, understand what is asked and why, decide it with what the plan offers, or in the operator's own words, and never decide by accident.
Trigger:: a decision of a plan or an epic is delivered to the operator: a worker asked (link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-envelope-schema[Question Envelope]), a server rule raised it, or a decision quorum escalated it because its confidence stayed below the threshold the decision declaration sets (link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-declarations[Decision Declarations]); or a worker files a waiver request.
Preconditions:: the operator has a client open, or is reached by a notification of one: the terminal client's notification while it runs (`console.notify`), the web app's browser notification once the operator allowed it.
Flow::
+
[source,text]
----
  [S] decision.open            the decision is delivered as the workflow made it:
       │                       what is asked and why now, the evidence, what it
       │                       blocks, how reversible it is, its parts with their
       │                       options, and what the workflow supplies: a
       │                       recommendation, the votes and dissent of a quorum
       ▼
  [S] decision.announce        every open client of the operator shows that it
       │                       waits, with its plan and what it blocks; none
       │                       takes the operator's focus, and nothing typed
       │                       before it was shown answers it
       ▼
  [O] decision.answer          per part: choose an option, give a value, or
       │                       answer in the operator's own words, with an
       │                       optional note; or for the whole decision: none
       │                       fits ("think again"), or later
       ├── own words ─────────► [M] decision.interpret   a role task maps the
       │                             │                   words onto the parts:
       │                             │                   an option, an option with
       │                             │                   a condition, a new option
       │                             │                   in the operator's words,
       │                             │                   "you decide", or unclear
       │                             ▼
       │                        [O] decision.read-back   "I understood: …";
       │                             │                   confirm, edit the words,
       │                             │                   or choose an option
       │                             ├── unclear ──► [O] decision.clarify
       │                             │                    one question, then
       │                             │                    decision.interpret again
       │                             ▼ confirmed
       │                        decision.answer, the part answered
       ├── answered in another channel ──► decision.closed   every client says
       │                                                     where and how
       ▼ every required part answered
  [O] decision.confirm         the whole answer, part by part, with what follows
       │                       from it; asked where the decision has several
       │                       parts or is not reversible
       ▼
  [S] decision.record          validated and recorded with its channel; the
       │                       first valid answer wins; until then the plan
       │                       waits, without a time limit
       ▼
  decision.closed
----
Variations::
* *What the workflow decides, the channel carries.* Whether a decision reaches the operator at all, whether a quorum voted on it first, the threshold below which it escalates, whether it carries a recommendation, whether own words are allowed, which role interprets them and how often it may ask back: each is modelled in the plan's workflow units and decision declarations, and every client shows and answers every variant the units can produce, never adding a rule of its own.
* *Parts depend on each other*: a part that depends on another is offered once that part is answered, and changing the earlier answer reopens it.
* *The interpretation sees the operator's words, the question, and the options, never the recommendation or the votes*, so that it does not lean towards them; nothing is recorded before the operator confirmed the read-back, and words that fit no option are recorded as the operator's own option, in the operator's words.
* *A waiver request* is announced the same way; it is answered by a waiver, which is an operator override and never a content answer, so it has no preselection and no interpretation.
* *No time limit*: either the quorum is confident and decides, as its declaration says, or the operator decides, and the plan waits until the operator has; no decision expires and none is taken by the passing of time (<<change-operator-wait>>). A client that was not open learns of the open decisions when it opens.
Outcome:: the plan goes on with the operator's decision, in the operator's words where the operator used them, or with the decision of a confident quorum; the record names the channel, the device, and whether the answer was chosen, interpreted and confirmed, or delegated.
Changes::
* *The decision structure* (<<change-decision-structure>>).
* *Answers in the operator's own words* (<<change-own-words>>).
* *Waiting for the operator* (<<change-operator-wait>>).
* *Answers from the terminal client* (<<change-cli-answers>>).
Requirements:: link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-12[PM-WF-12], link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7], link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-3[PM-SEC-3]; the question channels are specified in link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc[Model Work Specification, Questions], the quorum in link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc[Decision Quorum].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/decisions.adoc[Decisions] (what both clients show, for every variant); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/04-console.adoc#answering[Terminal Client, Answering a Question]; the web app's view is designed by its package `web-design`.; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/14-transcripts-decisions.adoc#t-decisions[Terminal Client, Decisions]

[#UC-PLN-3]
== UC-PLN-3: Design a Plan's Process

Status:: `DRAFT`, a stub: its building blocks are open points
Actor:: the operator.
Goal:: shape the process a plan runs, for example add a step to finalize, by arranging the building blocks the project declares, and see before it is applied whether the server accepts it.
Open points:: where a design lives (in the project's workflow units, in one plan, or in one plan with promotion to the project); whether a plan can be designed before it starts; which building blocks are offered; whether the terminal client offers it at all.
Requirements:: none yet: no requirement lets the operator change the process of a plan.

[#UC-PLN-4]
== UC-PLN-4: See What Runs, Across Projects

Status:: `DRAFT`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: see at a glance, across every project, where plans and epics are running and which of them need the operator, and get from there to the one that needs attention, without wading through projects that have nothing running or plans that have ended.
Trigger:: the operator opens the overview of running work; the client opens it where the operator left it.
Flow::
. *Projects.* The overview lists the projects that have running work: an active plan or epic, or something of the project that needs the operator without one (a question of its setup). A project without either is not listed, nor is an ended or archived plan. Each project shows whether something in it needs the operator, and how many plans and epics are active in it. Projects that need the operator come first, then the most recently active.
. *A project, by plan.* The project's active plans, each with: whether it needs the operator and for what (a decision, a waiver request, an integrity pause, stalled); its title; its current phase and its state in it, from one closed list: running, pausing, paused, waiting for the operator, stalled; since when it runs and since when it is in the current phase; its progress (tasks done of all); and the epic it belongs to, if any, from which the operator moves to that epic.
. *A project, by epic.* The project's active epics, each with whether it or one of its plans needs the operator, its title, since when it runs, how many of its plans run and how many are staged, and its plans below it as in the view by plan; the plans that belong to no epic follow as a group of their own, so that the two views show the same plans.
. *Into a plan or an epic*: selecting one leads into <<UC-PLN-1>>; a decision that waits leads into <<UC-PLN-2>>.
. *Where the operator left it*: the overview remembers the project, the view, and the selected plan or epic, and opens there the next time, per web browser and per terminal user; a selection that has ended falls back to its project.
Variations:: _Ended work_ lists the plans and epics of a project that ended (completed, cancelled, abandoned, archived), newest first, each leading to its view as it ended (<<change-ended-work>>). The overview follows the runtime as it changes: a plan that ends leaves it, a new one appears, an indicator changes when a decision arrives or is answered, without the operator asking for it. A plan and an epic are told apart by their icon, their colour, and a word, never by colour alone.
Outcome:: the operator knows where work runs and what needs the operator, and has reached the plan or epic to act on.
Changes::
* *Running work* (<<change-running-work>>).
* *Titles and names* (<<change-titles>>).
* *The start of the current phase* (<<change-phase-start>>).
* *What needs the operator, at a glance* (<<change-needs-operator>>).
* *The overview resource and its events* (<<change-overview-resource>>).
Requirements:: link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (the dependable view of every plan and epic); the runtime status is specified in link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], epics in link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc[Epic Coordination].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/plan-overview.adoc[Plan Overview] (what both clients show); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/15-overview.adoc[Terminal Client, The Overview].

[#UC-PLN-5]
== UC-PLN-5: Follow an Epic

Status:: `DRAFT`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: see an epic as a whole: what it starts from, how it is cut into workstreams and plans, where each plan is, what landed, what it found and watches, and what it waits for.
Trigger:: the operator selects an epic in Running Work (<<UC-PLN-4>>), or names it.
Flow::
. *The epic.* Its title, since when it runs, its state (running, waiting for the operator, held, landing), how many plans run at once of how many it allows, and its actions (link:06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-2[UC-CTL-2]).
. *Its workstreams.* Each workstream with its plans in the queue's order, each plan with its status (staged, running, pausing, paused, shipped, cancelled, abandoned, held), what it waits for (a dependency, a lock, the scope), and since when; a running plan leads into its plan view (<<UC-PLN-1>>).
. *Its sources.* What the epic starts from, as stored (link:05-starting-work.adoc#change-source-directory[The Source Directory]).
. *Its landings.* Each landed plan with its pull request and the epic's analysis of the landing.
. *Its findings and watches.* The open items with their counts, leading into the reviews (link:06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-3[UC-CTL-3]).
. *Its events.* What happened in the epic, newest first: plans started and ended, messages of its plans, acts of the operator, decompositions.
Variations:: the view follows the epic as it runs. An epic that ended shows as it ended, from Ended Work. An epic waiting for the operator (a decomposition, a held item, a decision) shows that first, leading to it.
Outcome:: the operator knows where the epic stands, plan by plan, and has reached the part to act on.
Changes::
* *The epic as a resource* (<<change-epic-resource>>).
* *Events per scope* (<<change-event-streams>>).
Requirements:: the epic is specified in link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc[Epic Coordination], its ledger in link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc[Epic & Change Ledger].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/epic-view.adoc[Epic View]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/19-actions.adoc#epic-view[Terminal Client, An Epic's View]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/web-app/README.adoc[Web App]

[#changes]
== Changes to the Specification

The changes these use cases need, each modelled as the fix of a gap; written into the requirements and specifications once the use case is `AGREED`.

[#change-phase-sub-workflows]
=== Phases as Sub-Workflows

Gap:: the seven phase files are merged into one plan unit; a phase is only the `phase` clause each state carries, transitions cross from one phase file into another, and the mapping of a state to its phase cannot be derived from its id (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#workflow-definition-units[Workflow Definition Units]; link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc[Phase Value vs State Identifier]). The process of a plan is therefore one flat graph: it cannot be shown phase by phase, a phase cannot be replaced or extended on its own, and a lane is a set of states rather than a choice of phases.
Fix::
* The plan unit is a workflow of phases: each phase (`init`, `refine`, `outline`, `plan`, `execute`, `land`, `finalize`) is a sub-workflow the plan unit calls, and its outcome decides the plan unit's next call. A lane is the plan unit's choice of which phases it calls; a loop-back is the plan unit calling a phase again; a re-entry from finalize into execute is the finalize phase ending with that outcome.
* A plan's phase is the phase sub-workflow on its call stack; the `phase` clause and the phase value table go.
* The plan instance composes the plan unit and the phase sub-workflows; a pruned phase is a pruned call, with its reason.
* The depth of the call graph and of `call_stack` grows by the phase level.
* This is an *intent change*: it changes how every phase is written, and it makes a phase a unit the composition, and the design of a plan's process (<<UC-PLN-3>>), can choose and replace.
Affects:: link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-16[PM-WF-16] (the call depth, also against the onboarding's nesting and a recipe's phases), link:../specification/store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc[Store Schemas] (the `phase` field of findings, of tasks in link:../specification/store-schemas/06-task-and-references.adoc[Task Records], and of link:../specification/store-schemas/17-re-planning.adoc[Re-Planning] records), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#workflow-definition-units[Workflow Definition Units], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc[Grammar] (the `phase` clause), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#static-verification-rules[Static Verification] (call depth), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/06-reusable-sub-workflows.adoc[Reusable Sub-Workflows], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-instance[Plan Instance], link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc[State Catalogue Conventions], every module of link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc[Phase Workflows], link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Workflow State Store] (`phase`, `call_stack`), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/07-loop-back-diagrams-verification.adoc[Loop-Back & Diagrams].

[#change-process-resource]
=== The Plan's Process as a Structure

Gap:: the composed process of a plan is available only as DSL text and as a diagram, and only over MCP (`pm://plan/{plan_id}/instance`, `pm://plan/{plan_id}/diagram`); its sub-units are named by digest and not expanded. A client cannot show it level by level, and no per-node state exists.
Fix::
* `GET /api/v1/plans/{plan_id}/process` returns the composed process of the version in force as a tree: the plan, its phases, every workflow's states in their order, and every call with the sub-workflow it calls, expanded, down to the leaves; each node with its id, its kind (phase, sub-workflow, state, and the kind of a state), and its *state*, derived by the runtime: `done`, `running`, `planned`, `waiting_for_operator`, `failed`, `not_taken`, or `not_in_plan` with its prune reason; with how often it ran and the run it is in, its first and last entry and exit times, and for a leaf its job or tasks.
* The path to the plan's current position is part of the answer, so a client can mark it and jump to it.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-instance[Plan Instance], link:../specification/hypermedia-format/01-representation-and-link-forms.adoc#call-path-breadcrumb[Call Path].

[#change-plan-history]
=== The Plan's History

Gap:: the plan store records where a plan is, not where it has been: apart from the firings of finalize steps, the gaps, and the closed phases of the metrics, nothing records which states and sub-workflows a plan entered and left, when, with which outcome, or which branch it took; the machine-wide audit log has transition records, but their details are not specified and no client can read them.
Fix::
* Each plan keeps an append-only history of its process: a state entered and left (with its outcome), a sub-workflow called and returned (with its outcome), the instance version in force, and the time; the runtime derives the states of the process structure from it.
* It is retained with the plan and archived with it.
Affects:: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Workflow State Store], link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Archives], link:../specification/store-schemas/12-machine-stores.adoc#audit-log[Audit Log] (transition records).

[#change-process-events]
=== Events of the Process

Gap:: the plan's event stream announces "transitions" without names or payloads, so a client cannot update a node of the process without reading it all again.
Fix::
* The plan's event stream announces a state entered and left, a sub-workflow called and returned, a step firing opened and closed, and a new instance version, each with the node's id and its new state; a new instance version tells the client to read the process again.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API] (events); the event catalogue of link:../discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#foundations[Specification Changes for the Clients].

[#change-decision-structure]
=== The Decision Structure

Gap:: a question carries a text of at most 500 characters, 2 to 5 options, and one recommended option with one confidence and one rationale; a quorum's vote names only the option it chose. There is no place for the description and evidence of a decision, no confidence per option, no decision in several parts, no statement of what the decision blocks or how reversible it is, and no answer but an option of the list.
Fix::
* *Decision*: the question set becomes a decision of its own, with a stable id (`<scope_id>/<question_id>`, the id of its first question, no new identifier kind) and a revision: a title, a short header, a description with its evidence (files, diffs, findings, links), what it blocks (the plan, a task, nothing), how reversible its outcome is (reversible, costly, irreversible), and its parts.
* *Part*: each question of the set is a part with a stable id, its question, an answer type (one option, several options, a value), the options, and an optional dependency on options of another part; whether own words and notes are allowed is declared per part.
* *Option*: a stable key, a short label, a description of its trade-off, its consequences, an optional preview (a diff, a text), and its *support* where a quorum voted: how many votes chose it, from which roles and models, and the dissent with its reason. A self-reported confidence stays a detail of each vote; the clients never present it as the probability that an option is right.
* *Recommendation*: a field of the part (the option, its rationale, who recommends it), never a part of an option's label.
* *Answer*: per part the chosen options or the value, the operator's own words, and a note; for the whole decision also _none fits_ (the plan rethinks) and _later_; with the channel, the device, and the revision it answers, so that a stale answer from a second client is refused.
* *Events*: the runtime announces a decision opened, changed, answered, and closed, with its id and revision, on the event streams of the plan, the epic, and the workspace.
Affects:: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-envelope-schema[Question Envelope], link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record], link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc[Decision Quorum] (votes per option), link:../specification/store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc#decision-record[Decision Record], link:../specification/hypermedia-format/02-link-grammar.adoc#named-parameter-schemas[Named Parameter Schemas] (`question-envelope`, `question-answer`), link:../specification/mcp-tools/01-core-workflow-tools.adoc#question-waiver-facts[Question Facts], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API] (endpoints and events).

[#change-own-words]
=== Answers in the Operator's Own Words

Gap:: an answer must name one of the listed options; its optional text changes nothing (except the revision text of the outline review), and nothing maps the operator's words onto the decision.
Fix::
* Where the part allows it, the operator answers in own words. The decision enters `decision.interpret`: a role task declared by the decision's unit (by default a read-only role without tools) receives the question, the options, and the words, and never the recommendation or the votes; its result is closed: an option, an option with a condition, a new option in the operator's words, a delegation ("you decide"), or unclear, each with the passages of the words it rests on.
* The runtime checks the result before it is shown: the option exists, the quoted passages occur in the words. A result that fails the check is shown as unclear.
* `decision.read-back` shows the result in one sentence beside the operator's words; nothing is recorded before the operator confirms it. Unclear leads to `decision.clarify`, one question, at most as often as the unit allows, then back to the choice of an option.
* Words that fit no option are recorded as the operator's own option, in the operator's words; the interpretation never substitutes the closest option. A delegation is recorded as such, and only where the decision allows one.
* The operator's words are the operator's own and never screened (link:05-starting-work.adoc#change-local-trusted[What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]); a model that reads them later gets them as quoted data in its facts, never as instructions of the system.
* The interpreting role is the shipped `read_only` role `interpret`, qualified like every role on its corpus topic `own_words`.
Affects:: link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc[Questions] (answers), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#question-states[Question States] (the states of the interpretation), link:../specification/model-work/01-roles-and-configuration.adoc#default-roles[Default Role Set] (the interpreting role), link:../specification/store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc#decision-record[Decision Record] (chosen, interpreted, delegated).

[#change-operator-wait]
=== Waiting for the Operator

Gap:: the specification holds that no state of any unit waits for an operator decision (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#waivers-and-integrity[Waivers & Integrity]) and lets a state rest on links only in units the entry session drives (link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#session-driven-units[Session-Driven Units], rule 10 of link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#static-verification-rules[Static Verification]). Every wait for the operator is therefore an exception, defined where it occurs: each question class, the waiver request, the choices of the setup carry their own wait, deadline, mode, and fallback, in some 35 places. A rule about waiting, such as that the operator is never overruled by time, cannot be stated once, and the operator steps of the onboarding (link:01-onboarding.adoc#UC-ONB-1[UC-ONB-1] and its sub-workflows) and of a decision (<<UC-PLN-2>>) have no place at all.
Fix::
* *One mechanism.* Waiting for the operator is a normal behaviour of a state in every unit and every scope, defined once in the workflow language: a state may be an *operator wait*. It offers the operator's links over the local API to both clients, the only places of the operator (link:05-starting-work.adoc#change-clients-only[All Interaction in the Operator's Clients]), holds no worker and no model slot, has no time limit, and ends only by an act of the operator through a link, or by an override (`abort`). Questions and decisions, waiver requests, consents, and the choices of the project setup and of the onboarding are operator waits; every specification that has one refers to this definition and keeps only what is its own (the links, the facts), never a wait, a deadline, a mode, or a fallback of its own.
* *Who decides.* A decision is taken either by a quorum confident by the threshold its declaration sets, or by the operator in an operator wait; nothing is decided by the passing of time, and nothing by a rule that asks no one. The question modes `auto` and `none`, `questions.wait_seconds`, the deadline `expires_at`, the class fallbacks on expiry, the closure `assumed`, and the expiry of a waiver request go with the per-place definitions that carry them.
* *What halts.* An operator wait halts its own scope and nothing else: a plan or an epic waits, and every other plan, epic, and project keeps running; the status shows it as waiting for the operator, never as stalled. The one wait that halts the system is the machine scope's: while the onboarding is not complete, the machine is not configured, so no plan and no epic starts, while the onboarding itself and the operator's verbs work (link:01-onboarding.adoc#change-machine-scope[Machine Scope]).
* *A waiver request* is an operator wait like a question; it ends by `waive` or by a new `decline`, which records the outcome `declined` and lets the remediation unit take its next transition.
* *The question modes go*: `questions.mode` and its values go with the per-place waits; whether a quorum runs is said only by the decision's declaration, and an escalated quorum delivers its decision as an operator wait; the rule that a state never waits for an operator decision, the bounded waiver wait (`waiver_wait_seconds`) that gates `request_waiver`, the closure `assumed`, and the error code `question_expired` go with them.
Affects:: link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#waivers-and-integrity[Waivers & Integrity] (the principle), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#session-driven-units[Session-Driven Units], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#question-states[Question States], link:../specification/workflow-dsl/04-ast-node-model.adoc#static-verification-rules[Static Verification] (rule 10), link:../specification/state-catalogue/01-conventions-generic-conditions.adoc[State Catalogue Conventions], link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-delivery[Question Delivery], link:../specification/timeouts/01-job-budgets-and-waits.adoc#question-wait[Question Wait] and the waiver wait, link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record] and the waiver request, link:../specification/store-schemas/08-project-configuration-local.adoc#local-config[Operator Local Configuration] (`questions.*`), link:../specification/cli-and-security/02-credentials-and-operator.adoc#operator-waive[Waivers] (`decline`), link:../specification/runtime-model/04-sessions-and-workers.adoc#stalled-plans[Stalled Plans], link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc[PM-WF-12]; the other places that define a wait of their own are reduced to a reference to it. This is an *intent change*: it replaces a founding principle of the workflow language., and the places that state the opposite rule today: link:../specification/state-catalogue.adoc[State Catalogue] (overview), link:../specification/workflow-dsl/08-remediation-units.adoc[Remediation Units] (no operator wait, the bounded waiver wait), link:../specification/phase-workflows/01-planning-phases-1-2.adoc[Planning Phases 1–2] (plan outcomes), link:../specification/phase-workflows/07-questions-waivers-operator-influence.adoc[Questions, Waivers & Operator Influence] (modes, deadlines, conditions with a fallback), link:../specification/state-catalogue/02-plan-phases-1-3.adoc[Phases 1–3] and link:../specification/state-catalogue/03-plan-phases-4-5-land.adoc[Phases 4–5] and link:../specification/state-catalogue/06-setup-epic-other.adoc[Setup & Epic] (the fallback `assumed`), link:../specification/mcp-tools/03-resource-uris.adoc[Resource URIs], link:../specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc[Decision Quorum] (the delivery of mode `auto`), link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API] (`question_expired`), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc[PM-SEC-3] (never waits for an operator decision).

[#change-running-work]
=== Running Work

Gap:: nothing defines which plans and projects have running work. An aborted plan stays in the project's `plans/` with phase `aborted` (it is never archived), so a listing of live plans includes it, and the predicate closest to "active" is the `scope_active` refusal of `unenrol`.
Fix::
* A plan or an epic is *active* while it is not in a terminal state (`completed`, `handed_back`, `returned`, `plan.aborted`; `epic.archived`, `epic.aborted`); an aborted plan is not active, wherever its directory lies.
* A project has *running work* while it has an active plan or epic, or an open decision of its workspace scope (a `setup` question); a released or unenrolled project has none.
Affects:: link:../specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#state-catalogue-terminal[Terminal States], link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Archives], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API].

[#change-titles]
=== Titles and Names

Gap:: a plan has no title (the pull request's title is authored in refine or the light outline, so it is absent before), and a project has no display name; only its `project_id` and checkout path identify it. An epic's title lives only in its ledger header.
Fix::
* A plan gets a `title` at its creation, one line drawn from its request, which the refine or outline phase may replace with a better one; the pull request's title stays its own field.
* A project gets a `display_name` at enrolment, derived from its remote (owner and name) and changeable by the operator; the `project_id` stays the identifier.
* The runtime status carries the epic's title and creation time from its header, so that a client needs no ledger read.
Affects:: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Workflow State Store] (`status.json`), link:../specification/store-schemas/11-enrolment-store.adoc#enrolment-store[Enrolment Store], link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-header[Epic Header].

[#change-phase-start]
=== The Start of the Current Phase

Gap:: neither a plan nor an epic stores when its current phase began; the metrics record a phase only once it closed, and the creation time of a plan is only its `origin.stamped_at`.
Fix::
* `status.json` records `created_at` and `phase_entered_at` (set on every phase change, also on a re-entry), so that a client shows since when a plan runs and since when it is in its phase.
Affects:: link:../specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc[Workflow State Store], link:../specification/store-schemas/05-metrics-and-quality-report.adoc#metrics-store[Metrics Store].

[#change-needs-operator]
=== What Needs the Operator, at a Glance

Gap:: what needs the operator is spread over many sources: open decisions, waiver requests, integrity pauses, stalled plans, conditions that name an operator command, held items of an epic, a failed epic landing, workspace setup repairs. No flag combines them per plan, epic, or project.
Fix::
* The runtime derives for every plan, epic, and project a `needs_operator` summary: whether anything needs the operator, how many items, and their kinds from a closed list (`decision`, `operator_wait` (any other operator wait: a consent, a decomposition or review, an option asked when needed), `waiver_request`, `integrity_pause`, `stalled`, `condition`, `held`, `landing_failed` (an epic's landing, or the removal from git of moved documents), `findings` (new findings of an epic), `paused`, `setup`); an epic's summary includes its plans', a project's its plans', epics', and workspace's.
* The summary is derived on read and never stored, like `stalled`.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], link:../specification/runtime-model/04-sessions-and-workers.adoc#stalled-plans[Stalled Plans], link:../specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc[Epic Coordination].

[#change-overview-resource]
=== The Overview Resource and Its Events

Gap:: the runtime status is specified only as a list of contents; there is no resource per project, no count of active plans and epics, and no event for a condition raised or cleared, a phase change, an epic's plan moving from staged to running, or an item held for the operator.
Fix::
* `GET /api/v1/projects` lists the projects with running work, each with its `display_name`, its `needs_operator` summary, and its counts of active plans and epics; `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}` adds its active plans (title, phase, state, `created_at`, `phase_entered_at`, task progress, `needs_operator`, the epic it belongs to) and epics (title, `created_at`, counts of running and staged plans, `needs_operator`).
* The runtime-wide event stream announces a project gaining or losing running work, a plan or epic starting or ending, a phase change, a condition raised or cleared, an epic's plan changing its status, and an item held for the operator, each with the ids a client needs to update its overview without reading everything again.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API] (resources and events), link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[Epic Queue Row].

[#change-cli-answers]
=== Answers from the Terminal Client

Gap:: a content question is answered only in the web app (channel `web`) or through the entry session's answer link (channel `session`); "there is no CLI command that answers one". The operator who works in the terminal client cannot decide what a plan asks without switching to the browser, and learns of the question only there.
Fix::
* The terminal client is the second channel of content answers beside `web`, `cli` (the channel `session` goes with link:05-starting-work.adoc#change-clients-only[All Interaction in the Operator's Clients]): it follows the runtime's events while it is open, announces every open question record and waiver request of the operator's plans and epics, and answers a question through the same scope-qualified answer endpoint as the web app, validated the same way; the first valid answer wins, a later one is refused (`already_answered`).
* An answer from the terminal client is a content answer, never an override; a waiver request is answered by `waive`, as today. The terminal client keeps the operator checks of `pm-operator`.
* Every client says when a question was answered in another one, and withdraws what it showed.
Affects:: link:../requirements/08-security.adoc[PM-SEC-3, PM-SEC-7] (the web app is no longer the only dependable channel of content questions), link:../Requirements.adoc#PM-WF-12[PM-WF-12], link:../specification/cli-and-security/02-credentials-and-operator.adoc#operator-channel-isolation[Operator Channel Isolation] (item 1: "there is no CLI command that answers one"), link:../specification/cli-and-security/03-web-server.adoc#web-app[Web App] (the dependable question channel), link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API] (the answer endpoint for channel `cli`), link:../specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#question-record[Question Record] (the channel), link:../specification/store-schemas/18-decision-records.adoc#decision-record[Decision Record] and link:../specification/model-work/11-decision-records.adoc[Decision Records] (the channel enums), link:../specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc[Questions] (the channel rules), link:../specification/phase-workflows/07-questions-waivers-operator-influence.adoc[Questions, Waivers & Operator Influence] ("no content-answer command exists").

[#change-epic-resource]
=== The Epic as a Resource

Gap:: an epic is read through `pm://epic/{epic_id}/ledger` and `pm-operator status` only; the local API has no epic resource, no queue row with its status and what it waits for, no registers, no decomposition draft, no archive check, and no endpoint for the operator's acts on an epic.
Fix::
* `GET /api/v1/epics/{epic_id}`: the header (title, state, `parallelization_scope`, `needs_operator`), the workstreams with their rows (status, what it waits for, since when, the plan of a running row), the sources' manifest, the landings, the counts of the registers.
* Sub-resources: the registers of findings and watches, the decomposition draft while one is open, the archive check, the epic's history of events.
* The operator's acts on an epic as endpoints (next plan, decompose again, archive, cancel, a review's dispositions), validated like every operator act and answered with the new state.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], link:../specification/mcp-tools/03-resource-uris.adoc[Resource URIs] (`pm://epic/*`), link:../specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc#epic-queue-row[Epic Queue Row].

[#change-event-streams]
=== Events per Scope

Gap:: the local API streams events runtime-wide and per plan only (`GET /api/v1/events`, `/plans/{plan_id}/events`); an epic, a project's workspace, the machine scope, and a pending entry (a start, a project's onboarding) have no stream, though their operator waits and progress must reach both clients.
Fix::
* One event stream per scope: `GET /api/v1/epics/{epic_id}/events`, `/projects/{project_id}/workspace/events`, `/machine/events`, `/entries/{entry_id}/events`, with the same envelope, catalogue, and resume rule as the plan's; the runtime-wide stream keeps announcing every scope's start, end, and `needs_operator` change, so that the overview needs no other stream.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], the event catalogue of link:../discussions/client-specification-changes.adoc#foundations[Foundational Findings] (item 3).

[#change-ended-work]
=== Ended Work

Gap:: Running Work shows only what runs (<<change-running-work>>); an ended plan or epic, in the project's archive or among its plans with phase `aborted`, has no listing and no way into its view.
Fix::
* `GET /api/v1/projects/{project_id}/ended` lists the project's ended plans and epics, newest first, with their outcome, reason, and end time, paged; each resolves to its process and history as it ended, read from the archive.
Affects:: link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#local-api[Local API], link:../specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#archives[Archives].
