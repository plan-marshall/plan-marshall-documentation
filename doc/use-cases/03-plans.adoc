= Use Cases: Plans (UC-PLN)
:toc: left
:toclevels: 2
:nofooter:

link:../UseCases.adoc[Use Cases] » Plans

The operator works with running plans and epics: follows them down to the part that needs attention, decides what a plan asks, shapes the process a plan runs, and catches up on what happened while away. The operator does this in the web app or in the terminal client, except designing a process, which only the web app offers (<<UC-PLN-3>>); each use case says what the operator achieves, and how each client shows it is its design (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/README.adoc[`pm-mcp-clients`, Design]).

[#UC-PLN-1]
== UC-PLN-1: Follow a Plan Down to Its Parts

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: follow a plan from its phases down to the step it is in, see at every level what is done, what runs, and what is still planned, and understand where it is and how it got there.
Trigger:: the operator selects a plan in the overview of running work (<<UC-PLN-4>>), or names it.
Flow::
. *The plan.* The plan's phases, in the order the plan runs them, each with its state: _done_, _running_, _planned_, _waiting for the operator_, or _failed_; the phases its composition left out (another lane, a step the project does not need) are listed apart as _not in this plan_, with the reason. A phase the plan ran more than once (a loop-back) says how often, and the run it is in. The plan says the tokens it used so far, with how many went in and how many came out, and how many of its tasks were not measured; never a cost and never a limit (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles & Bundle Manifest Specification, Tokens, Not Money]).
. *A phase.* Selecting a phase shows its workflow: its states in the order of the workflow, each with its state (the states of a phase, and _not taken_), and the sub-workflows it calls marked as sub-workflows, each with its state as a whole. Where the workflow branches, the branch taken is shown, and a branch not taken as _not taken_. A step that failed shows _failed_, with its outcome.
. *A sub-workflow.* Selecting a sub-workflow shows its workflow the same way, and so on through every level of nesting, down to the leaves: the states that call nothing.
. *A leaf.* A leaf shows what runs or ran in it: its job or its tasks (role, worker, attempt, outcome), its times, and its outcome; a state waiting for the operator leads into <<UC-PLN-2>>.
. *A worker at work.* A task that runs shows what its worker does, as it does it: its activity, newest last, each entry with its time: what the model wrote, each tool it called with the target (a file, a command, a link of the task such as `submit:TASK-003`), whether the call failed, the files it changed, and the questions it asked; above it the task's role, harness, model, attempt, how long it runs, and the tokens it used so far. A task that ended shows its activity as it was. What the worker read from the project or from outside is shown as text, never acted on, and secrets in it are masked. Where the server judged the worker off track, the task says so, with the signal and what the server did about it (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work Specification, A Worker Off Track]).
. *Where it is.* At every level the path from the plan down to the level shown is visible, and the way back up; the level the plan is at is marked, and the operator can jump to it.
Variations:: the view follows the plan as it runs: a state that is entered becomes _running_, one that is left _done_, and a new version of the plan's composition (an outline, a replan) changes what is planned, which the view shows as changed. A plan whose process changed after a state ran shows that state as it was when it ran. This use case is about plans; an epic has a view of its own (<<UC-PLN-5>>). *One step to its project and its epic*: from a plan, its project and its epic, if it has one, are each one step away, wherever the plan is shown (Running Work, the project, the epic, a decision, ended work); a plan opened from its epic's view leads back to the epic in that one step, and from an epic its project is one step away (<<UC-PLN-5>>). The way back to where the plan was opened stays as it is.
Outcome:: the operator knows, at every level of the plan's process, what is done, what runs, and what is still to come, and has found the part that needs attention.
Requirements:: link:../requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11] (the plan's process as a structure from its history, what each worker does), link:../requirements/16-model-work.adoc#PM-WORK-7[PM-WORK-7] (worker activity and off-track signals), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-16[PM-WF-16] and link:../requirements/14-implementation.adoc#PM-IMPL-5[PM-IMPL-5] (a plan as a workflow of phases), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14] (tokens used, never a cost), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-1[PM-SEC-1] (activity as untrusted text), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (both clients as dependable views); specified in link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-resource[The Plan's Process as a Structure], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#process-events[Events of the Process], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#plan-history[The Plan's History], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/03-findings-and-review-records.adoc#phase-sub-workflows[Phases as Sub-Workflows], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-instance[Plan Instance], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#worker-activity[What a Worker Does], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[A Worker Off Track], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Tokens, Not Money].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/plan-view.adoc[Plan View] (what both clients show); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/16-plan-view.adoc[Terminal Client, The Plan View]; the web app: no design document yet; designed by its package link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/plans/web-design.adoc[`web-design`]

[#UC-PLN-2]
== UC-PLN-2: Decide What a Plan Asks

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#clients-only[Workflow DSL & Engine Specification, All Interaction in the Operator's Clients]).
Goal:: learn at once that a plan, an epic, a project's setup, or a start waits for a decision, understand what is asked and why, decide it with what is offered, or in the operator's own words, and never decide by accident.
Trigger:: a decision of a plan, an epic, a project's workspace scope (a question of its setup), or a start's entry is delivered to the operator: a worker asked (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-envelope-schema[Question Envelope]), a server rule raised it, or a decision quorum escalated it (its roles disagreed, one stayed below the declaration's confidence, or the quorum could not decide, link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc#decision-declarations[Decision Declarations]); or a worker files a waiver request.
Preconditions:: the operator has a client open, or is reached by a notification of one, once the operator allowed it (how each client notifies is its design).
Flow::
+
[source,text]
----
  [S] decision.open            the decision is delivered as the workflow made it:
       │                       what is asked and why now, the evidence, what it
       │                       blocks, how reversible it is, its parts with their
       │                       options, and what the workflow supplies: a
       │                       recommendation, the votes and dissent of a quorum
       ▼
  [S] decision.announce        every open client of the operator shows that it
       │                       waits, with its subject (the plan, the epic,
       │                       the project's setup, or a start's entry) and
       │                       what it blocks; none takes the operator's
       │                       focus, and nothing typed before it was shown
       │                       answers it
       ▼
  [O] decision.answer          per part: choose an option, give a value, or
       │                       answer in the operator's own words, with an
       │                       optional note; or for the whole decision: chat,
       │                       none fits ("think again"), or later
       ├── chat ──────────────► ↳ chat (UC-CTL-1) over the decision, with
       │                         the role of the task that asked, or the
       │                         subject's planning role; it never answers:
       │                         an answer it proposes is offered as own words
       │                         ─► decision.interpret; else ─► decision.answer
       ├── own words ─────────► [M] decision.interpret   a role task maps the
       │                             │                   words onto the parts:
       │                             │                   an option, an option with
       │                             │                   a condition, a new option
       │                             │                   in the operator's words,
       │                             │                   "you decide", or unclear
       │                             ▼
       │                        [O] decision.read-back   "I understood: …";
       │                             │                   confirm, edit the words,
       │                             │                   or choose an option
       │                             ├── unclear ──► [O] decision.clarify
       │                             │                    one question, then
       │                             │                    decision.interpret again;
       │                             │                    bound reached ──►
       │                             │                    decision.answer, the
       │                             │                    part unanswered
       │                             ▼ confirmed
       │                        decision.answer, the part answered
       ├── none fits ─────────► [S] decision.withdraw   the decision is closed
       │                             │                   withdrawn; the asking
       │                             │                   role thinks again (a
       │                             │                   quorum's decision is
       │                             │                   voted again; offered
       │                             │                   only where one of them
       │                             │                   can)
       │                             ▼
       │                        decision.open, the decision asked anew
       ├── later ─────────────► decision.announce   the decision stays open;
       │                                            the client leaves it, and
       │                                            its subject waits
       ├── answered in another channel ──► decision.closed   every client says
       │                                                     where and how
       ▼ every required part answered
  [O] decision.confirm         the whole answer, part by part, with what follows
       │                       from it; asked where the decision has several
       │                       parts or is not reversible
       ▼
  [S] decision.record          validated and recorded with its channel; the
       │                       first valid answer wins; until then its
       │                       subject waits, without a time limit
       ▼
  decision.closed
----
Variations::
* *What the workflow decides, the channel carries.* Whether a decision reaches the operator at all, whether a quorum voted on it first, the threshold below which it escalates, whether it carries a recommendation, whether own words are allowed, which role interprets them and how often it may ask back: each is modelled in the plan's workflow units and decision declarations, and every client shows and answers every variant the units can produce, never adding a rule of its own.
* *Parts depend on each other*: a part that depends on another is offered once that part is answered, and changing the earlier answer reopens it.
* *The interpretation sees the operator's words, the question, and the options, never the recommendation or the votes*, so that it does not lean towards them; nothing is recorded before the operator confirmed the read-back, and words that fit no option are recorded as the operator's own option, in the operator's words.
* *None fits, or later.* _None fits_ closes the decision withdrawn, with the operator's note: the role that asked thinks again and asks anew, as a new decision. A decision no role asked (a server rule raised it, a quorum escalated it, the setup asked it) offers _none fits_ only where a quorum is declared, which then votes again; otherwise _none fits_ is not offered, and the decision stays open (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[Model Work Specification, The Decision Structure]). _Later_ answers nothing: the decision stays open and announced, the client leaves it, and the plan waits.
* *A waiver request* is announced the same way; it is answered by `waive` or by `decline`, which records the outcome `declined` and lets the remediation unit go on (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL & Engine Specification, Waiting for the Operator]). Both are operator overrides and never a content answer, so a waiver request has no preselection, no interpretation, and no chat.
* *A chat before answering* asks back ("what does option B mean for the open pull request?") with the role of the task that asked, or, for a decision a server rule raised or a quorum escalated, with the subject's planning role (`replan` for a plan, `decompose` for an epic); it may explain the recommendation and the votes, which the interpretation of an answer it proposes still never sees. A revision of the plan it proposes goes through the guards of a plan's chat; the decision stays open, or is closed _withdrawn_ with the reason when the revision removes what asked it. The plan waits during the chat as for any decision; an answer in another channel closes the decision and ends the chat (link:06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-1[UC-CTL-1]; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/state-catalogue.adoc#chat[State Catalogue, Chatting with the Planning Role: `chat`]).
* *A project's setup asks*: a decision of the workspace scope (a question of the project's setup, link:04-projects.adoc#UC-PRJ-1[UC-PRJ-1]) is announced with its project, shown in the project's running work (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[State Catalogue, Running Work]), and answered the same way; it holds the setup only, never a plan. A chat over it is with the role of the task that asked; a question the setup's workflow raised has no planning role, and its chat is offered disabled with that reason.
* *No time limit*: either a quorum decides as its declaration says (unanimous, each vote at or above its score, calibrated), or the operator decides, and the plan waits until the operator has; no decision expires and none is taken by the passing of time (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Workflow DSL & Engine Specification, Waiting for the Operator]). A client that was not open learns of the open decisions when it opens.
Outcome:: the plan, the epic, or the setup goes on with the operator's decision, in the operator's words where the operator used them, or with the decision of a confident quorum; the record names the channel, the device, whether the answer was chosen, interpreted and confirmed, or delegated, and whether it followed a chat.
Requirements:: link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-12[PM-WF-12] (decisions, answers, own words, both channels), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-20[PM-WF-20] (waiting for the operator, no time limit), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-18[PM-WF-18] (recommendation and quorum), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-19[PM-WF-19] (the record of how it was answered), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-24[PM-WF-24] (a chat before answering), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-3[PM-SEC-3] (content answers apart from overrides), link:../requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11] and link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (both clients announce and answer); specified in link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#decision-structure[The Decision Structure], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#question-delivery[Delivery to the Operator], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/06-envelopes-questions-deadlines.adoc#own-words[Answers in the Operator's Own Words], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#operator-wait[Waiting for the Operator], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/02-units-jobs-decisions.adoc#question-states[Question States & Decisions], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/14-plan-instance-waivers-questions.adoc#cli-answers[Answers from the Terminal Client], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/state-catalogue/16-decision.adoc#state-catalogue-decision[the decision unit's states], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/10-decision-quorum.adoc[Decision Quorum].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/decisions.adoc[Decisions] (what both clients show, for every variant); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/04-console.adoc#answering[Terminal Client, Answering a Question], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/14-transcripts-decisions.adoc#t-decisions[Terminal Client, Decisions]; the web app: no design document yet; designed by its package link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/plans/web-design.adoc[`web-design`]

[#UC-PLN-3]
== UC-PLN-3: Design a Plan's Process

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app.
Goal:: shape the process plans run, for example add a step to finalize or change how a remediation decides, by writing a workflow unit the operator may own; start it from a file, a pasted text, or a unit already installed; see before it is placed whether the server accepts it and what process it gives; and place it for this machine or for a project.
Trigger:: the operator opens the designer in the web app: from the machine's settings or a project's (to design for the machine or for that project), or from a plan's process (<<UC-PLN-1>>), where the unit a phase or a sub-workflow comes from is one step away. This use case is bound to the web app by its nature: designing a process means reading and editing a unit of the workflow language beside its structure, which a terminal does not show well, so the terminal client offers no designer and points to the web app (as link:02-entry.adoc#UC-ENT-1[UC-ENT-1] is bound to the terminal client).
Preconditions:: the machine is onboarded. A unit for the machine is designed with no project chosen, from the machine's building blocks; a project to validate against and to place in is enrolled and set up; without one, the operator can design and validate, and place at machine level only once a project exists to compose against.
Flow::
+
[source,text]
----
  [O] designer.open            the units the operator may own, each with the
       │                       level in force (the project's copy, the
       │                       machine's copy, or the default): the phase
       │                       units, remediation.*, replan,
       │                       adjudicate.decisions, git.commit_and_push, the
       │                       variants of the plan axes (landing.*, method.*,
       │                       ask.*), and the recipes of the machine and
       │                       of the project; the compiled-only units are
       │                       shown read-only; choose one, or a new recipe
       ▼
  [O] designer.input           what it starts from: an uploaded .pmwf file, a
       │                       pasted text, or an installed unit (the
       │                       project's copy, the machine's, or a copy of
       │                       the default); nothing of it runs here
       ▼
  [O] designer.edit            the unit as text, with line numbers, and as
       │                       its structure (its states, the phases and
       │                       sub-workflows it calls, outcomes and
       │                       transitions), kept in step, and its
       │                       frontmatter (name, description, type,
       │                       entries and exits, author); the building
       │                       blocks of the server's catalogue, every
       │                       element the language accepts (primitives,
       │                       guards, links, conditions, sub-workflows,
       │                       roles, parameters, keywords), each inserted
       │                       as text
       ├── validate ────────► [S] designer.validate   parsed, verified by the
       │                             │                static rules and the
       │                             │                compiled invariants, and
       │                             │                composed in a dry run
       │                             │                against a chosen project:
       │                             │                findings with rule, line,
       │                             │                and column, marked in the
       │                             │                structure; when valid,
       │                             │                the process it gives
       │                             ▼
       │                        designer.edit
       ├── leave ───────────► done   nothing is placed; the server keeps
       │                             no draft
       ▼ place
  [O] designer.place-target    this machine (every project without a copy of
       │                       its own) or a project (its team's copy)
       ▼
  [S] designer.validate-target  again, against the target: for a project
       │                       that project; for the machine every project
       │                       in which the unit would be in force
       ├── findings ─────────► designer.edit, the findings marked
       ▼ valid
  [O] designer.confirm         the difference against the unit it replaces
       │                       at that level (or that there is none), and
       │                       the process it gives; confirm, or back
       ├── back ─────────────► designer.edit
       ▼ confirmed
  [S] designer.place           re-validated; refused if the unit it replaces
       │                       changed meanwhile; for the machine written to
       │                       its store (a recipe beside its recipe in
       │                       recipes/); for a project written to
       │                       .marshall/config/ (a recipe beside its recipe
       │                       in .marshall/recipes/) and landed by a short
       │                       landing (one commit, landed as the project
       │                       lands; with landing.files left uncommitted in
       │                       the checkout)
       ▼
  done                         the unit is placed at its level; a plan takes
                               it once its own checkout or worktree has it
----
Variations::
* *What can be designed*: a unit the operator may own, and only such: the project-owned units (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/state-catalogue.adoc[State Catalogue], Default units), the variants of the plan behaviour axes (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/09-plan-instance.adoc#plan-axes[Workflow DSL & Engine Specification, Plan Behaviour Axes]), and the recipes (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/domain-extensions/05-recipes-and-extension-points.adoc#recipe-workflow[Domain Extensions, Build Map & Configuration Split Specification, A Recipe Is a Workflow]), which are placed, like every designable unit, for the machine or for a project: a machine's recipe is offered in every project on this machine, and a project's recipe of the same key takes its place there. The compiled-only units (the epic, the setup, the lesson maintenance, the machine and the project onboarding, the bootstrap and source units, `chat`, `review`, `decision`, `git.land-unit`, the entity units) are building blocks: shown read-only, offered for a call, never replaced; an input that names one is refused at placing. An axis has the variants it defines; a variant is changed, never added.
* *The input is the operator's own*: an uploaded file and a pasted text are the operator's content, trusted like code (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/07-untrusted-text-screening.adoc#local-trusted[Model Work Specification, What Is on This Machine Is Trusted]), and nothing of them runs before the unit is validated and placed; the dry run starts and stores nothing.
* *The building blocks are what the server accepts*: the catalogue offers every element a unit can use, of the machine or of a chosen project, from the same registries the server validates against, so a block it offers is one validation accepts; it changes with the runtime's version and with what is placed, and the designer follows it.
* *The frontmatter*: the designer shows and edits the unit's frontmatter beside its text (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/workflow-dsl/01-grammar.adoc#unit-frontmatter[Unit Frontmatter]): its name and description, its type, and its entries and exits with their descriptions. On an edit it sets the modification date to the day and the version to the runtime's release, and drops a signature, which would no longer cover the content; the author is the operator's name, asked once, and never `plan-marshall`. A unit that replaces another keeps its type, entries, and exits (`INV-GEN-1`); a changed interface is a finding at its line.
* *Text and structure in step*: an edit of the text redraws the structure; a text that does not parse keeps the last structure, marked out of date, with the parse finding at its line. No model writes or changes the unit.
* *A unit with findings* can be edited on and validated again, never placed. A finding names its rule or compiled invariant (`INV-*`), its line, and its column; a finding of the dry run names the project and what failed there (a role not defined, a skill not resolved).
* *Placing at machine level* puts the unit in force for every project on this machine that has no copy of its own: the project's copy wins over the machine's, the machine's over the default. A recipe placed for the machine is written beside its recipe in the machine's `recipes/` and offered in every project on this machine, unless the project has a recipe of the same key. The machine's units and recipes are the operator's and never committed. Placing is refused while the unit fails in any project in which it would be in force, each such project named with its findings.
* *Placing at project level* writes the team's copy into the project (`.marshall/config/<unit>.pmwf`, a recipe's workflow `.marshall/recipes/<id>.pmwf` beside its recipe) and lands it by one commit, as the project lands (`landing.pull-request` or `landing.main`); with `landing.files` it stays uncommitted in the checkout. Until it has landed, the landing shows in the project's running work, and a failed landing waits for the operator like a moved document's removal (link:05-starting-work.adoc#UC-STA-3[UC-STA-3]).
* *Placing over an existing unit* shows the difference first and asks; an unchanged text is not placed. If the unit at that level changed after the designer read it, placing is refused and the operator sees the new one.
* *Removing a placed unit*: from the units in force, the operator removes the machine's or the project's copy; the unit of the next level (the machine's copy, then the default) is validated against the projects concerned, its difference shown and confirmed, and the removal is placed the same way (at project level a short landing).
* *When a plan takes it*: a project unit is in force for a plan once the plan's own checkout or worktree has it. A new plan starts from the base branch, so it takes the unit once it has landed there; a running plan gets it with its worktree's next sync with the base branch and adopts it then through its adoption at the next phase handshake, where the new version verifies and contains the plan's current position, and keeps the version in force otherwise (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/domain-extensions/06-configuration.adoc#config-snapshot[Three-Surface Synchronization]). With `landing.files` the file is only in the operator's checkout, and no plan takes it until it is committed. A machine unit reaches every plan of the projects it applies to at their next phase handshake, through the same adoption.
* *At phone width* the web app shows the unit read-only, with validate (its design says how).
Outcome:: the unit is placed where the operator placed it, taken by the plans as their checkout or worktree has it, validated against every project it applies to; or nothing changed.
Open points:: designing a unit for one plan only, and promoting it to the project, is not offered now; nor is a model's help in designing.

[#UC-PLN-4]
== UC-PLN-4: See What Runs, Across Projects

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: see at a glance, across every project, where plans and epics are running and which of them need the operator, and get from there to the one that needs attention, without wading through projects that have nothing running or plans that have ended.
Trigger:: the operator opens the overview of running work; the client opens it where the operator left it.
Flow::
. *Projects.* The overview lists the projects that have running work: an active plan or epic, or something of the project that needs the operator without one (a question of its setup). A project without either is not listed, nor is an ended or archived plan. Each project shows whether something in it needs the operator, and how many plans and epics are active in it. Projects that need the operator come first, then the most recently active.
. *A project, by plan.* The project's active plans, each with: whether it needs the operator and for what (a decision or another operator wait, a waiver request, or a hold: stalled, a condition, an integrity pause); its title; its current phase and its state in it, from one closed list: running, pausing, paused, waiting for the operator, held; since when it runs and since when it is in the current phase; its progress (tasks done of all); whether one of its workers is off track (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/03-supervision-and-recycling.adoc#worker-off-track[Model Work Specification, A Worker Off Track]), which needs no act and is shown apart from what needs the operator; and the epic it belongs to, if any, from which the operator moves to that epic.
. *A project, by epic.* The project's active epics, each with whether it or one of its plans needs the operator, its title, since when it runs, how many of its plans run and how many are staged, and its plans below it as in the view by plan; the plans that belong to no epic follow as a group of their own, so that the two views show the same plans.
. *Into a plan or an epic*: selecting one leads into <<UC-PLN-1>>; a decision that waits leads into <<UC-PLN-2>>.
. *Where the operator left it*: the overview remembers the project, the view, and the selected plan or epic, and opens there the next time, per web browser and per terminal user; a selection that has ended falls back to its project.
Variations:: _Ended work_ lists the plans and epics of a project that ended, newest first, each with how it ended: a plan with its outcome (merged, no change, handed back, returned, cancelled, abandoned, released, or aborted from an integrity pause), an epic with its end (completed, cancelled, released), each leading to its view as it ended and to a read-only chat about it, from which a follow-up plan may start (link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Ended Work]). The overview follows the runtime as it changes: a plan that ends leaves it, a new one appears, an indicator changes when a decision arrives or is answered, without the operator asking for it. A plan and an epic are told apart by their icon, their colour, and a word, never by colour alone.
Outcome:: the operator knows where work runs and what needs the operator, and has reached the plan or epic to act on.
Requirements:: link:../requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11] (running work, `needs_operator`, the ended work of a project), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-23[PM-WF-23] (pausing and the holds), link:../requirements/16-model-work.adoc#PM-WORK-7[PM-WORK-7] (the off-track indicator), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-15[PM-WF-15] (epics and their plans), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (both clients as dependable views); specified in link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#overview-resource[The Overview Resource and Its Events], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#needs-operator[What Needs the Operator, at a Glance], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/state-catalogue/04-plan-finalize-terminal.adoc#running-work[Running Work], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#titles[Titles and Names], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/02-workflow-state-store.adoc#phase-start[The Start of the Current Phase], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[Ended Work], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[Events per Scope].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/plan-overview.adoc[Plan Overview] (what both clients show); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/15-overview.adoc[Terminal Client, The Overview]; the web app: no design document yet; designed by its package link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/plans/web-design.adoc[`web-design`]

[#UC-PLN-5]
== UC-PLN-5: Follow an Epic

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: see an epic as a whole: what it starts from, how it is cut into workstreams and plans, where each plan is, what landed, what it found and watches, and what it waits for.
Trigger:: the operator selects an epic in Running Work (<<UC-PLN-4>>), or names it.
Flow::
. *The epic.* Its title, since when it runs, its state (running, waiting for the operator, held, landing), whether it starts its plans by itself (automatic on or off), how many plans run at once of how many it allows, the tokens it used so far (its plans' and its own tasks', with how many went in and how many came out; link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles & Bundle Manifest Specification, Tokens, Not Money]), and its actions (link:06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-2[UC-CTL-2]).
. *Its workstreams.* Each workstream with its plans in the queue's order, each plan with its status (the queue row's status, with pausing, paused, waiting for the operator, and held shown from its plan), what it waits for (a dependency, a lock, the scope, an overlap with a running plan), and since when; a running plan leads into its plan view (<<UC-PLN-1>>).
. *Its sources.* What the epic starts from, as stored (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/filesystem-layout/03-project-store.adoc#source-directory[Filesystem Layout Specification, The Source Directory (`sources/`)]).
. *Its landings.* Each landed plan with its pull request and the epic's analysis of the landing.
. *Its findings and watches.* The open items with their counts, leading into the reviews (link:06-controlling-work.adoc#UC-CTL-3[UC-CTL-3]).
. *Its events.* What happened in the epic, newest first: plans started and ended, messages of its plans, acts of the operator, decompositions.
Variations:: the view follows the epic as it runs. An epic that ended shows as it ended, from Ended Work, with a read-only chat about it (link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Ended Work]). An epic waiting for the operator (a decomposition, a held item, a decision) shows that first, leading to it. From the epic, its project is one step away, and from each of its plans the epic (the rule of <<UC-PLN-1>>).
Outcome:: the operator knows where the epic stands, plan by plan, and has reached the part to act on.
Requirements:: link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-15[PM-WF-15] (the epic, its queue, and the plans it starts), link:../requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-5[PM-ARCH-5] (its git-tracked ledger), link:../requirements/01-architecture.adoc#PM-ARCH-11[PM-ARCH-11] (the epic as a resource and its events), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-23[PM-WF-23] (its actions), link:../requirements/02-workflow.adoc#PM-WF-14[PM-WF-14] (the tokens it used), link:../requirements/08-security.adoc#PM-SEC-7[PM-SEC-7] (both clients as dependable views); specified in link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#epic-resource[The Epic as a Resource], link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#event-streams[Events per Scope], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/model-work/08-epic-coordination.adoc[Epic Coordination], link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/store-schemas/04-epic-and-change-ledger.adoc[Epic & Change Ledger].
Design:: link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/shared/epic-view.adoc[Epic View] (what both clients show); link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/design/terminal-client/19-actions.adoc#epic-view[Terminal Client, An Epic's View]; the web app: no design document yet; designed by its package link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-clients/blob/main/doc/plans/web-design.adoc[`web-design`]

[#UC-PLN-6]
== UC-PLN-6: Catch Up on What Happened

Status:: `AGREED`
Actor:: the operator, in the web app or in the terminal client.
Goal:: learn what happened in the projects, epics, and plans since the operator last looked (a plan started or ended, an epic started its next plan or found an overlap, a project onboarded), in a structured way, without having to act on any of it, and get from each to its details.
Trigger:: the operator enters a client (logs in, opens the web app, starts the terminal client), which shows how many reports are unread and leads to them in one step; or a new report's notice while a client is open.
Flow::
+
[source,text]
----
  [S] reports.list             the unread reports, newest first, grouped by
       │                       project, then epic and plan; each with where
       │                       it comes from (the project, the epic, the
       │                       plan, each one step away), its subject, when
       │                       it happened and when it was sent, and whether
       │                       it leads to a detail
       ▼
  [O] reports.choose           open a report's detail, mark it read, mark
       │                       all shown read, or leave
       ├── detail ───────────► [S] reports.detail   the report read; its
       │                             │              detail shown: a plan's
       │                             │              completion report, an
       │                             │              epic's landing or its
       │                             │              completion review, the
       │                             │              plan at its re-planning,
       │                             │              the project
       │                             ▼ back
       │                        reports.list
       ├── mark read ────────► [S] reports.read     the report read, on every
       │                             │              device ─► reports.list
       ├── mark all read ────► [S] reports.read     every report shown read;
       │                                            one that arrived since
       │                                            stays unread ─► reports.list
       ▼ leave
  done                         the unread reports stay unread
----
Variations::
* *A report never asks*: it tells what happened. What needs the operator stays where it is asked, a decision (<<UC-PLN-2>>), another operator wait, or a hold, shown in Running Work (<<UC-PLN-4>>); a report may point at it, never answer it.
* *Several devices*: a report is read once for the machine; read on one device or client, it is read on every other, and a list open elsewhere drops it.
* *While a client is open*, a new report appears with a quiet notice, like a decision's announcement but never blocking and never taking the operator's focus; the list, if shown, gains it at its place.
* *The subject has gone*: a report of a plan or an epic that has ended and is archived leads to its view as it ended (link:../specification/runtime-model/01-processes-and-transport.adoc#ended-work[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification, Ended Work]); one of a released project to what is kept of it.
* *None unread*: entering a client says so, and the list is empty; read reports are not shown again.
* *What a plan used*: a plan's completion report says what the plan used in tokens, in total with how many went in and how many came out, beside its wall time; by phase, by role, and by model and harness; what was spent on tasks that produced nothing and on retries and remediation; and how many tasks were not measured, a total over them shown as at least its value. It shows no cost and no limit: the plan's usage is reported, never held against a budget (link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/client-profiles/01-profile-schema.adoc#token-usage[Client Profiles & Bundle Manifest Specification, Tokens, Not Money]).
* *Kept*: an unread report never expires; a read one is kept until the operator deletes it in the cleanup (link:07-system.adoc#UC-SYS-3[UC-SYS-3]).
* *Work history can be deleted*: the report `system.cleanup_due` comes from the machine, not from a project; its detail is the cleanup job (link:07-system.adoc#UC-SYS-3[UC-SYS-3]). The detail of a report whose plan or epic the cleanup deleted says "deleted" with the date.
Outcome:: the operator knows what happened while away, has reached the details that matter, and has no unread report left that the operator does not want to keep unread.
