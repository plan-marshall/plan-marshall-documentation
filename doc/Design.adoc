== Overview

The design shows how the link:UseCases.adoc[Use Cases] look and behave on the screen: the views, what they show, how one moves between them, the interactions, and the visual language. It is the counterpart of the link:Specification.adoc[Specification] for what a person sees; the specification states what the system does behind it, and a design never contradicts it.

== Conventions

* *One document per subject*: `design/NN-<subject>.adoc`, listed in <<index>>.
* *Traceability*: every document names the use cases it serves (`_Use cases:_` below its title) and the specification sections it relies on.
* *Status*: `DRAFT` while it is being worked out, `AGREED` once the operator agreed to it.
* *Pictures*: wireframes and mock-ups are SVG files in `doc/resources/`, named `design-<subject>-<what>.svg`, embedded with `image::`; a picture always has a text that says what it shows.
