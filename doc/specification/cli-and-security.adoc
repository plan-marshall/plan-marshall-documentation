== Overview

This document specifies the two command-line binaries of *plan-marshall-mcp* (`PM-MCP`), cut by caller (`pm-mcp`, the worker-facing relay `serve --job`; `pm-operator`, the terminal client, one of the operator's two clients, with its entry and console), the installation layout of the four release binaries, and the machine-level security mechanisms around the runtime: cross-process `FileChannel.lock()` coordination, the contract the two clients of the operator share (operator choices, answers, and acts in the web app and the terminal), the automatic onboarding, the client bundle installer (`pm-operator client`) and its drift checks, the operator's credential acts, operator channel isolation and the operator acts (including the `roles` verbs, the ends of plans and epics, and the integrity pause), and the optional web access the runtime serves to paired browsers on a loopback and a LAN listener (`pm-operator web`). Token-lane credentials with the credential stores and the operator-chosen store, provider verification, a project's onboarding, enrolment, and release, the security audit trail, and outbound HTTPS are specified in the link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/credentials-and-enrolment.adoc[Credentials & Enrolment Specification].

The process topology (always-on runtime, `pm-mcp serve --job` STDIO relay of workers), the local transport and the local API with its operator endpoints and event streams, `stdout` hygiene, and the workers are specified exclusively in the link:runtime-model.adoc[Runtime & Interaction Model Specification]; runtime start, stop, restart, and upgrade and crash recovery in the link:https://github.com/plan-marshall/pm-mcp-core/blob/main/doc/specification/runtime-lifecycle.adoc[Runtime Lifecycle Specification].
